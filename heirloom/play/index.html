Heirloom

Everybody means to record them. Almost nobody does.

The formal interview never gets scheduled. The voice notes go unlabelled. Then it is too late, and the sentence is always the same: I wish I had recorded her.

The method

One question a week. That is the whole thing.

Record your life story is paralysing. Tell me about your first job is answerable in six minutes. Heirloom hands over one question at a time and keeps every answer in their own voice. Every story adds a ring.

Where it lives

On this phone, and nowhere else.

There is no account and no cloud. Heirloom cannot open a network connection at all. Recordings stay in this app until you export them, and the export is a normal folder of audio files you can copy anywhere, or bring back into Heirloom on another phone.
