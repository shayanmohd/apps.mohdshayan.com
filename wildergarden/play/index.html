Our pledge

A subscription to help you use your phone less would be a conflict of interest. So there isn't one.

  • No subscription. No unlock, no premium tier, no cosmetic shop. All four biomes and all forty species are in the app you already have.
  • No advertising. There is no ad code in the build and no advertising identifier is read.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into, nothing to delete later.
  • No internet. The app does not request the internet permission, so Android will not let it open a connection at all. It also opts out of Android backup and device transfer, so the system cannot copy your ledger off the phone either.
  • No surveillance. Wildergarden never reads which apps you use. It asks you once a day and believes you.
  • One notification. At a time you choose, to close the day. Nothing is designed to pull you back in.
  • No streak shaming. Grace covers the first slip every week. Recovery is always faster than the damage. That ratio is deliberate.
