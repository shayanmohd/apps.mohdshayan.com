Kith

Who are your people?

Not your contacts. Not your followers. The handful you would call from a hospital, and the wider circle you would hate to lose.

Kith keeps them in four rings, each with its own rhythm, and quietly tells you when one has drifted. Nothing is uploaded. There is no account to make.

Start with five.

Type a name, choose a ring. You can add the rest later, and change any of it whenever you like.

    What Kith can never see

    • Your contacts. Every name here was typed by you.
    • Your calls and messages. Kith has no permission to read either, so a catch up is only logged when you say so.
    • The internet. The app does not hold the permission, so it cannot open a connection even by accident.
    • Anything about the other person's side. Kith records what you give. It never tracks whether they replied.

    Your notebook

    An export is a plain file written to your Downloads folder by your own tap. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you would keep a diary.
