Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy for SocialSure: the website at www.socialsure.co and the SocialSure Android app (com.socialsure.app) on Google Play.

Effective Date: 25/01/2026
Last updated: 06/11/2026

1. INTRODUCTION

SocialSure Pvt. Ltd. (“we,” “us”) values your trust. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. THE SOCIALSURE ANDROID APP

The Android app opens the SocialSure web app at https://www.socialsure.co full screen, using Chrome on your device (a Trusted Web Activity). Everything you do in the app happens on the SocialSure website, so this policy covers the app and the website in the same way.

  • The app itself contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It does not read your contacts, location, photos, files, camera or microphone.
  • It asks for one permission: notifications (Android 13 and later), so that SocialSure can show notifications through the app. You can turn this off at any time in Android settings.
  • All connections between the app and SocialSure use HTTPS (TLS encryption).

3. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect the following categories of Data Principals' (User) information:

  • Identity Data: Name, Email, Phone Number (optional), Digital Signature details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture; when you sign in with an emailed link we receive your email address.
  • Profile Data: For creators: niche, content style, platforms, follower range, social media link, preferred brands and agency information. For brands: company name, industry, description, logo, website, authorized representative, contact email, contact phone and business address.
  • Agreement Data: For contracts and Payment Protection forms: the names and contact details of the creator and the brand, deliverables, payment amounts and due dates, the date and place of execution, and your signature with the time and the IP address it was signed from.
  • Professional Data: PAN Card, GST Number and Bank Account details, only when you provide them for KYC verification or a Payment Protection case.
  • Content You Create: Deal proposals and applications and the messages in them, community posts, comments and likes, and the questions you send to the AI Legal Bot.
  • Payment Data: Your subscription plan and status. Card, UPI and bank details you enter at checkout go directly to our payment processor, Razorpay; we do not store them.
  • Creator Gifting: If you connect a Shopify store, an access token for that store. If you claim a gift, your name, email address, social media handle and shipping address, which go to the brand that sends the gift.
  • Careers: If you apply for a job, your name, email, phone number, college, year of study, portfolio link, the reasons you give and your resume.
  • Usage and Performance Data: Pages viewed, referring page, device type, browser, operating system, country, and page loading speed, collected by Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights. These tools use no cookies and are not linked to your account.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

We process your data for the following specific purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To generate legally binding contracts and Payment Protection forms, run the marketplace, and send you notifications and emails about your deals and agreements.
  • Account Management: To sign you in, keep your account secure, and manage your subscription.
  • AI Matching: To train our proprietary matching engine to connect Creators with Brands based on performance metrics and the profile details you enter.
  • Risk Assessment: To audit accounts for the "Quarterly Digital Risk Audit" (Elite Plan).
  • Analytics: To understand which pages are used and how fast they load, so we can improve SocialSure.
  • Legal Compliance: To verify identity (KYC), keep evidence of who signed an agreement and when, and prevent fraud.

We do not show advertising and we do not use your data for advertising.

5. AI AND AUTOMATED PROCESSING

  • Disclosure: We use Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) technologies to process your data.
  • Chatbot: Your queries are processed by LLMs (Google Gemini) to generate responses. Do not share sensitive personal confessions or trade secrets with the bot.
  • Matching: Our algorithms automatically profile your social media performance and profile details to suggest brand deals.

6. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

We do not sell your data. We share data only in the following ways:

  • With Counterparties: Your KYC and contact details are shared with the specific Brand or Creator you enter into an agreement with, apply to, or send a proposal to.
  • With Other Users: Community posts and comments, and the marketplace profile details you enter, are visible to other SocialSure users.
  • With Legal Partners: If you request a human consultation or escalation, your case details are shared with our empanelled lawyers.
  • With Service Providers: Companies that run parts of SocialSure for us and may only use the data to do so: Vercel (hosting, file storage, Web Analytics and Speed Insights), Cloudflare (network delivery), our database hosting provider, Google (Google sign-in and the Gemini AI model), Razorpay (payments), Resend (email delivery) and Shopify (creator gifting, only if you connect a store).
  • Blockchain Record: When an agreement is notarized, a cryptographic hash of the signed document (not the document or your personal details) is recorded on the Polygon blockchain, where it is public and cannot be removed.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose data if required by Indian law enforcement or court order.

7. DATA SECURITY

We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) to protect your data during transmission. Digital signatures and timestamps on contracts are stored using secure hashing protocols to ensure evidentiary value under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

8. HOW TO DELETE YOUR SOCIALSURE ACCOUNT AND DATA

You can delete your SocialSure account at any time, in the Android app or on the website:

  1. Sign in to SocialSure (in the app or at https://www.socialsure.co).
  2. Open Dashboard > Settings.
  3. Under Delete Account, tap Delete Account, type confirm delete account and confirm.

If you cannot sign in or no longer have the app, email contact@socialsure.co from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my SocialSure account". We will delete the account and its data and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your account and sign-in records, your profile, posts, comments and likes, your proposals and applications, your notifications, your connected gifting stores, and the Payment Protection forms and agreements you created as a creator, from our database.

What may remain: agreements in which you are the brand stay with the creator, with the brand details written in them, because they are the creator's legal record; copies of signed agreements already sent to the other party; the document hash on the blockchain; payment records that Razorpay must keep by law; and usage and performance data, which is not linked to your account.

9. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a summary of your personal data processed by us.
  • Correction: Update or correct your personal data.
  • Erasure: Delete your account and data as described in section 8.
  • Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent for AI processing, but this may limit your access to the Marketplace and Chatbot.
  • Grievance Redressal: Contact our Grievance Officer for any privacy concerns.

10. COOKIES

We use cookies to keep you signed in. Our analytics tools do not use cookies. Razorpay's checkout may set its own cookies when you pay. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.
