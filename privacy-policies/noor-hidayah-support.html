Help during a trip

Your operator and group leader are your first contacts for travel arrangements or a problem during the trip. If a complaint remains unresolved for 15 days, email SOSA at the address above. Noor Hidayah is not an emergency service; contact local emergency services directly for urgent assistance. In the current testing service, staff see an SOS only while their dashboard or Partner app is open.

Report a problem

Tell us the app name, screen, language and what you expected to happen. For a ritual, dua or translation correction, identify the text and screen. Do not send passport or visa scans, Aadhaar numbers, passwords, sign-in codes or screenshots containing other people's personal information.
