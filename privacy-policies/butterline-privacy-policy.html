Butterline Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Butterline: Plane Landing Game (com.mohdshayan.butterline) is a free game published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what happens to your information in the Android app and in the web version at https://apps.mohdshayan.com/butterline/play/.

Data we collect

None. Butterline has no account, no analytics or crash reporting of our own and no server of our own, and the game itself sends nothing about you anywhere. The game is paid for by ads that Google serves. Google's ads and billing software in the app collects some data, described below, and that data goes to Google, not to us.

Ads

The Android app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google: full-screen ads at natural breaks between landings, never during an approach or a run, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch. It does not show banner ads. To choose and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and shares with Google your device's IP address (which can estimate your general location), how you interact with the app and its ads (such as launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information (such as launch time and performance), and device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID. This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Privacy options in the game's menu. If you do not consent, you can still play; Google may then show non-personalised or limited ads, which still use your IP address and diagnostic information.

Rewarded ads

Rewarded ads are only shown when you choose to watch one for a stated reward, such as flying a botched Daily approach again, keeping a Circuits run going after a bad landing, or flying the Daily airport a second time in another aircraft. They are always a bonus: you can play on without one.

Purchases

Remove ads is a one-time purchase made in the Google Play Store app under Google's terms, through Google Play Billing. It removes the ads between landings for good and gives the rewarded bonuses (Go around, Keep the run and the second daily round) at once without an ad, at the same limits. The app itself only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns it (and confirms a new purchase to Google Play, as Play requires), keeps that answer on your device and sends nothing else. We never see your payment details. Google Play Billing, the Google library that talks to the Play Store app, also sends Google diagnostic details about the purchase service, such as your device model, Android build, language, country, network type and the app's version. Once the app has confirmed that you own Remove ads, it requests no more ads and no longer shows the consent form; buying it does not erase ad data Google has already received. If you reinstall the game, Google Play restores the purchase on the same Google account.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the game hands a short text with your result (the day number, your landing stamps and any go-around mark), a link to https://apps.mohdshayan.com/butterline/play/ and, optionally, an image of the result card to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. On the web it uses your browser's share sheet, or copies the text to your clipboard where sharing is not available. The game itself sends nothing anywhere: what is sent, and to whom, is up to you and the app you choose, under that app's own privacy terms. Sharing needs no permission.

On the web

On the web (https://apps.mohdshayan.com/butterline/play/), the game page shows ads through Google AdSense. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Ads Settings (https://www.google.com/settings/ads). Other ad technology providers that Google works with may also serve or measure ads on the page; Google lists them at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903, and you can opt out of many of them at https://www.aboutads.info/choices/. Where required, Google's consent message asks for your choice first, and its "Privacy and cookie settings" link on the page lets you change it. The web version has no account, no purchases, no full-screen or rewarded ads and no analytics of ours.

Game progress

Game progress is saved only on your device: in the app's storage (and your device's own backup, if you use it), or on the web in your browser's local storage. We have no account system and keep no data about you on any server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes your progress; on the web, clearing this site's data in your browser does.

Permissions

The released Android app requests exactly these permissions:

  • INTERNET: lets the ads SDK load and show ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets the ads SDK check whether a network is available.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: lets the ads SDK read the Android advertising ID.
  • WAKE_LOCK: used by the ads SDK's background task scheduler to finish its work.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: used by the same ads SDK task scheduler; the game runs no foreground service of its own and shows no notifications.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets the app ask the Google Play Store app to sell and confirm Remove ads.

It asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, phone or notification permission.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, precise location or payment details, and there is no account to create. The app contains no analytics or crash reporting SDK of our own. Nothing the game does reaches a server of ours: we run none for it. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ads and billing software sends to Google (on the web, Google AdSense), described above, and anything you choose to share.

Children

The game is meant for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.
