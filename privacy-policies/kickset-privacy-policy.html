Kickset Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Kickset is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Kickset collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. Jobs, saved cuts and settings you create are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. Android's own backup service is switched off for Kickset: the app turns Auto Backup off and excludes every one of its data folders from cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so the system does not copy your jobs into your Google account either. You can export them to a file you choose with Back up to file, or as a CSV or PDF cut sheet, and you delete them by deleting a job in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app. Optional local counts of solves, saves and exports are off unless you turn them on in Settings, and they also stay on the device.

Permissions

Kickset asks for no device permission: no internet, no storage, no location, no camera and no microphone. Because it has no internet permission the app itself cannot send your data off the device, and with Android's backup service switched off for it there is no system path off the device either. Play will show no device permissions for this app. The one entry in the app's manifest is a signature-level permission that Android's own support libraries add for their internal use (com.mohdshayan.kickset.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION); it is scoped to Kickset's own signature, grants nothing to any other app, and collects nothing.

Third parties

Kickset contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app includes the Google Play In-App Review library. If Google Play shows a rating prompt and you choose to leave a review, the review is handled by the Google Play Store app under Google's own terms; Kickset never receives it.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes
