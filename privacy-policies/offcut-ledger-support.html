Offcut Ledger support

Android 8.0 or later is required. All features are included in the one-time purchase.

Start a cutting project

Choose New project and select linear stock or rectangular sheets. Enter the material, thickness, kerf and edge allowances. Add available stock and required parts. Choose Find cutting layouts to compare alternatives. Grain constraints and locked rotation can prevent a part from fitting even when its area is small enough.

Enter dimensions

Use explicit units such as 450 mm, 45 cm, 0.45 m or 17 3/4 in. Bare numbers use your selected dimension preference. Fractions may have denominators up to 1,024. Calculations retain 0.001 mm precision; displayed fractional inches round to 1/64 inch and use an approximation mark when needed.

Read a plan

Numbered regions are required parts. R labels identify remaining offcuts. Coordinates in the cut sequence are measured from the original stock's top-left corner. Trim the stated edge allowances first. Plans use straight guillotine cuts and are the best found by the app's searches, not a mathematical guarantee. Read the dimensions rather than measuring the diagram.

Record a completed job

Choose Record completed job only after checking the plan. Enter the smallest offcuts you want to retain. Recording deducts used saved offcuts, adds qualifying remainders and preserves the exact plan in History. New stock is a project input, not a separately tracked inventory balance. A changed or missing saved offcut requires a fresh plan.

Export, share or print

At the bottom of a plan, choose A4 or US Letter, then export the PDF plan or labels. Share plan opens Android's share sheet. Print plan and Print labels use Android's print interface; choose portrait A4 or US Letter. Printer margins reduce the schematic to the printable area. Printed diagrams are not full-size cutting templates. PDF labels expand to fit complete part names.

Move or restore records

In Settings, choose Export full backup and save the file somewhere you trust. On the destination device, choose Restore backup, select that file and confirm the replacement. Validation happens before any records are replaced. Backups have a 32 MB limit and are not encrypted. Export before deleting older records to stay within the 200-project, 2,000-offcut and 500-job limits.

A CSV import creates a new project. It does not consume offcut inventory or preserve another project's live inventory references. CSV exchange uses the app's versioned columns; exporting a small project gives you a valid example to edit.

A file cannot be opened or saved

Check available storage and choose another destination in the Android picker. A removed file or revoked provider access requires selecting the file again. If Android restarts the app while an export picker is open, start the export again. Existing workshop records remain available.

Privacy and deletion

There is no app account or developer cloud. See the privacy policy for local storage, exports and deletion. Android Settings can clear all local app records; exported files must be deleted separately.
