Privacy Policy for Bhakti Time

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Bhakti Time (com.socialsure.bhakti) is a devotional app for daily bhakti routines, devotional audio, rituals, muhurat guidance, festival preparation, reminders, share cards and an optional AI devotional guide. It is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what happens to your information in the Android app. It is linked from Settings > Privacy in the app.

1. Summary

We, SocialSure Private Limited, receive no data about you. Bhakti Time has no account, no analytics or crash reporting of our own and no server of our own. Your settings stay on your device. Data leaves your device in three ways, and in each case it goes to Google, not to us:

  • Ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) shows banner ads and collects and shares with Google the data described in section 2.
  • Ask Bhakti Guide. Only when you ask the guide a question, the question is sent to Google's Gemini API to write an answer (section 4).
  • Premium. Google Play Billing handles the Premium subscription and sends Google diagnostic details about the purchase service (section 5).

In the terms of the Data safety section on Google Play, the app collects and shares approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics, and device or other IDs, for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance; diagnostics also for app functionality (Google Play Billing); and, only if you use Ask Bhakti Guide, the questions you ask (other user-generated content), for app functionality. All of it is encrypted in transit.

2. Ads

Unless you have Premium, Bhakti Time shows a small banner ad at the end of the Home and Festivals screens through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google. To choose and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects and shares with Google your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), how you interact with the app and its ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK (such as crash logs, app launch time, hang rate and energy usage), and device and account identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on your device. This data is encrypted in transit with TLS. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Android settings (on many phones under Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads), and manage ad personalisation at https://www.google.com/settings/ads.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Other ad technology providers that Google works with may also serve or measure ads in the app; Google lists them at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903.

3. Your consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app (version 1.3.1 and later) asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and stores your choice on your device. Where a privacy law gives you a choice (those regions and some US states), a Privacy options entry in the app's Settings lets you change your consent or opt out at any time; the entry appears only where it applies. If you do not consent, you can still use every feature of the app; Google may then show non-personalised or limited ads, which still use your IP address and diagnostic information. Premium users see no ads, so the app does not ask them.

4. Ask Bhakti Guide (AI devotional guidance)

Ask Bhakti Guide is optional. When you type a question, or tap one of the suggested questions, the app sends that question, with an instruction to answer in your chosen language (English or Hindi), over an encrypted HTTPS connection to Google's Gemini API, which writes the answer. Google also receives your device's IP address with the request. The app does not send your name, account or any identifier of ours with the question, and it does not save the conversation: it is cleared when you leave the screen. We do not receive your questions or the answers.

Google processes questions and answers under the Gemini API terms (https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). Depending on the service tier, Google may use them to improve its products and human reviewers may read them; Google also keeps them for a limited time to detect abuse. Please do not enter private, sensitive, medical, legal, financial or personally identifying information. AI answers are general devotional and informational support only, not professional, medical, legal, financial or guaranteed astrological advice.

5. Premium and purchases

Bhakti Time Premium is an optional subscription sold in the Google Play Store app under Google's terms, through Google Play Billing. It removes the ads. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account has the subscription (and confirms a new purchase to Google Play, as Play requires), keeps that answer on your device and sends nothing else. We never see your payment details. Google Play Billing, the Google library that talks to the Play Store app, also sends Google diagnostic details about the purchase service, such as your device model, Android build, language, country, network type and the app's version. You can manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store app at any time.

6. Settings, reminders and audio

The app keeps your settings in its private storage on your device: your language and theme, the interests and reminder time you chose at the start, which reminders are on, your favourite mantras, the last five tracks you played, and whether Premium is active. They are used only to make the app work for you and are never sent anywhere. Reminders are scheduled on your device with Android's alarm service, so no server is involved. The devotional audio is packaged inside the app and plays without the internet.

7. Notifications

On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission to show notifications, which it uses only for the devotional, festival, muhurat and streak reminders you leave on. You can turn each reminder off in the app's Settings, or turn notifications off in Android settings.

8. Sharing

When you share a blessing or a good morning card, the app hands the text, or a picture it draws on your device together with a short caption, to the app you pick in Android's share sheet, or straight to WhatsApp if you choose that and it is installed. The picture is kept in the app's private cache until the next one replaces it. What is sent, and to whom, is up to you and the app you choose, under that app's own privacy terms. The app never posts anything by itself.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or to delete on request. Your settings stay on your device until you remove them: clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app deletes them. Data that Google's ads SDK, the Gemini API and Google Play Billing receive is kept by Google under its own retention rules, described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's settings. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as we need it to answer you.

10. Security

Everything the app and Google's software in it send is encrypted in transit (TLS). Your settings and the app's record of Premium are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Purchases are handled by the Google Play Store app, so the app never sees your payment details.

11. Permissions

The released app requests these Android permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: shows your reminders (Android 13 and later ask you first).
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: lets the app schedule your reminders with Android's alarm service.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: puts your reminders back after your phone restarts.
  • INTERNET: lets the ads SDK and Google's consent form load ads and consent messages, lets Ask Bhakti Guide get answers, and lets Google Play Billing send its diagnostics.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: lets the app and the ads SDK check whether a network is available.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: lets the ads SDK read the Android advertising ID.
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS: added by the ads SDK so that, on devices that offer Android's ad services (Privacy Sandbox), it can use their advertising ID, attribution reporting and Topics features. You can turn these off in your device's privacy settings for ads.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets the app ask the Google Play Store app to sell and confirm the Premium subscription.
  • WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE: used by Android's background task scheduler (WorkManager), which the ads SDK uses to finish its work; the app runs no foreground service of its own.

It also declares one permission of its own, com.socialsure.bhakti.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, which Android's support library (AndroidX) adds so that other apps cannot send messages to the app's internal receivers. It gives the app no access to your data or to any device feature.

It asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, phone or SMS permission.

12. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you write to us), phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or payment details, and there is no account to create. The app contains no analytics or crash reporting SDK of our own, and nothing it does reaches a server of ours: we run none for it. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ads, Gemini and billing services receive, described above, and anything you choose to share.

13. Children

Bhakti Time is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that we can help.
