Free and lifetime features

Unlimited manually written cards, spaced repetition, three PDF notebooks, search, card exchange and complete backups are free. The free library overview can show contextual AdMob banners. One optional lifetime unlock removes ads and adds unlimited PDF notebooks, image occlusion, batch region creation and custom daily study plans.

Existing cards and originals remain accessible, editable, studyable and exportable if ownership is unavailable. New premium creation tools and custom-plan execution require ownership. Basic daily study continues to include existing premium-created cards.

Cards and PDF notebooks

Choose a deck in Library, then import a PDF or write a card. PDFs are copied privately, up to 100 MiB and 10,000 pages. Remove document passwords before import. Use page controls and zoom to read, then attach a card to the displayed page. Select a region by tapping opposite corners or entering percentage coordinates; that image region can be the answer. A card without an answer image needs a written answer.

Batch region creation makes one card for each region and the question you enter. It does not perform OCR or generate questions. Image occlusion accepts supported JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIF images, preserves orientation and creates one card per mask. During study, all labels are covered; revealing exposes that card's target region. Up to 100 regions can be created in one batch.

Study and progress

Daily study offers up to 20 new cards and 100 due reviews. Review cards are presented first. Reveal the answer, then choose Again, Hard, Good or Easy. The next interval is shown before grading. Again keeps the card due today; finish the session and begin another to revisit it. The schedule uses your device's local calendar date.

Pause preserves the current card and whether its answer is revealed. Draft edits are saved locally as you type. Resume a draft from Study or Library. Custom plans choose decks, daily limits and review order. Previous reviews in those decks count toward the limits. Progress counts activity, not proficiency.

Import, export and backup

Import UTF-8 CSV or tab-separated text up to 8 MiB. Named columns are front, back and optional deck. Without a header, use question, answer and optional deck, in that order. Quote fields containing delimiters or newlines. Text imports add cards without replacing the existing collection.

CSV and text exports are readable written content, not complete study backups. Image answers, original files and schedules require the complete ZIP backup. Save one in Settings before changing phones. Archives are not encrypted and Android automatic backup is disabled.

Restoring replaces the current library only after validation. Limits are 1 GiB expanded content, 32 MiB of metadata and 100 MiB per original. Leave enough free space for a staged archive and a second copy of its originals. Invalid archives leave the saved library intact. Purchase ownership, preferences and unfinished drafts are not included.

Purchases, ads and deletion

Google Play displays the localized lifetime price before purchase. Restore using the account that bought it. Pending payments unlock only after confirmation. Confirmed ownership is available offline; refunds or revocations can remove premium creation access after the next ownership refresh. Google handles payment and refund requests.

Advertising privacy choices appear in Settings where supported. Declining consent does not block study records. Unlocking stops new ad requests. Delete individual cards, decks or notebooks inside the app; clear app storage or uninstall to remove everything, including history. Delete exported files separately.
