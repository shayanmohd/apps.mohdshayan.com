Common questions

Is this gambling?

No. It is a parcel-drafting and spatial-packing strategy game. There is no real-money betting, currency purchase or cash-out.

Can I use taps instead of dragging?

Yes. Select a parcel, rotate it if needed, then tap the bag cell where its top-left corner belongs.

What happens when I miss a target?

The run ends, and the final manifest shows why. Start another seed or replay the same route with different decisions.

Does a run survive closing the app?

Yes. Every draft choice, placement and upgrade is saved locally, including the undo history before dispatch.
