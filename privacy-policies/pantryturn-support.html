What is free?

Pantry records, recipes, shopping lists, local barcode memories, entered-date reminders, basic exports and complete archive backup/restore are free. One lifetime purchase adds weekly meal planning, expiry-first ingredient matching, portion scaling and reusable shopping plans. Existing records stay readable, deletable and exportable without ownership.

Why does a new barcode need a name?

PantryTurn has no online product catalogue. The first time you save a code, enter its product name, unit and storage location. Later scans recall that local mapping. They do not copy a previous quantity, date or note. You can enter codes manually if the camera is unavailable or permission is denied.

Dates and matching

Copy a package date or enter your own record using YYYY-MM-DD. PantryTurn does not calculate shelf life or decide if food is safe. Ingredient matching uses exact names after normalizing case and spaces. It uses g/kg/mg, ml/l and each conversions, and treats custom units literally. Cups, cans and weight are not automatically interchangeable.

For a planned day, earlier eligible dates are allocated first and past dates are excluded. Weekly calculations share stock across all planned meals. Recording cooking recalculates availability before any deductions. You can also record a meal without changing pantry amounts.

Missing reminders

Enable reminders in Settings and allow notifications when asked. Check the app's Android notification settings if permission was previously denied. Reminders are a daily summary after the chosen local hour. Android power management can delay them, and force-stopping an app may prevent background work until it is opened again. No exact alarms are used.

Backup and restore

Use Settings to save a complete ZIP archive to a destination you choose. Store it safely because it is not encrypted. The archive contains pantry records, recipes, lists, meal plans, shopping plans and local barcode memories. Settings and purchase/reviewer ownership are excluded.

Restoration replaces the local records only after full validation. Invalid or damaged archives leave existing records intact. Import limits are 32 MiB expanded archive content and 100,000 records. Pantry CSV imports are limited to 8 MiB, append new records and create duplicates if repeated. Export a pantry CSV first to see the required version marker and columns. Recipe text and meal/shopping CSV exports are readable records rather than full restore formats.

Purchase and restoration

Open Settings, then Buy or restore lifetime unlock. Google Play supplies the localized price. Use Restore purchase with the Google account that bought the unlock. Pending payments do not unlock features until Google confirms completion. Confirmed ownership remains available offline. Refunds or revocation may remove planning access when Play next refreshes ownership; saved records remain accessible.

Google Play handles payment and refund requests. We cannot guarantee purchase approval, payment completion or a particular refund outcome. Reviewer access is separate from purchase ownership and is not a purchase trial.

Delete your data

Delete individual records from their detail screens. To remove all app records, settings, local barcode memories and drafts, clear PantryTurn storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Delete exported files separately wherever you saved them. Google Play purchase records remain with Google.
