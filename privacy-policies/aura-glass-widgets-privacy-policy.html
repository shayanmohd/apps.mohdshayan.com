Privacy Policy for Aura Glass Widgets

Effective date: 9 July 2026

Aura Glass Widgets ("the app") is a paid home screen widget pack. This policy explains

what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: the app has no

accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it keeps your data on your device.

Who we are

Developer contact: shayanm2002@gmail.com

Data stored on your device only

The following is created and stored locally on your phone (using Android Room and

DataStore) and is never uploaded to us or anyone else:

  • Quick notes, to-do items, focus or Pomodoro sessions, and your "one thing today" history
  • Your theme choice (accent color, light or dark mode, Material You on or off)
  • Per widget settings such as the weather city, world clock cities, and focus timer length
  • A daily step baseline used to compute today's step count

This data stays on the device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

The only network request: weather

To show weather, the app sends the coordinates (latitude and longitude) of the city you

choose to the Open-Meteo public weather API (https://open-meteo.com) over an encrypted

HTTPS connection, and receives the forecast in return. If you turn on the optional "use my

location" setting, your approximate (coarse) location is used to look up those coordinates.

No account, device identifier, name, email, or advertising ID is sent. Weather responses are

cached on the device for about 30 minutes. Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy practices

apply to that request.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Internet and network state: fetch weather and avoid requests when offline.
  • Approximate location (coarse), optional: only if you enable "use my location" for the

    • weather widget. Typing a city name never requires this.

  • Calendar (read only): the next event widget shows your upcoming event. Calendar data

    • is read on the device and is never transmitted.

  • Activity recognition: the step widget reads the hardware step counter on the device.
  • Notifications: the focus timer can post a "session complete" notification.
  • Notification listener (media access), optional: the music widget uses Android's media

    • session access to show and control whatever is currently playing. The app does not read,

    store, or transmit the content of your notifications. You enable this yourself in Settings

    and can turn it off at any time.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not require an account or sign in.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because your content lives only on your device, you control it. Uninstalling the app or using

Android's "clear data" removes it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new

effective date.
