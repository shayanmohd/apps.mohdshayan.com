Cropmark Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

Cropmark is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Cropmark collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. The app has no internet permission, so nothing it holds can be sent anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Cropmark stores, in its private app storage on your device: the photos you take or choose, the face measurements and person outline it works out from them, your edits, a record of the files you saved, any custom sizes you add and your settings. Face detection and background removal run on the phone with models built into the app. None of this is uploaded, and photos are excluded from Android cloud backup.

You can delete one photo from its page in History, or everything from Settings with Delete all photos. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Settings also offers Back up photos, which writes one zip file to a place you pick, and Restore backup, which reads that file back.

Form files and print sheets you save go to Pictures/Cropmark or Documents/Cropmark, or to the app you share them with. Saved files carry no location or camera metadata.

Permissions

Cropmark requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: takes the passport photo with the live measuring frame. Camera frames are checked in memory to place the guides and are never stored; only the photo you take is saved, on the device.

Photos from your gallery are chosen with the system photo picker, which needs no storage permission.

Third parties

Cropmark contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data. After you have saved photos on three separate days, the app may ask Google Play to show its rating sheet once; anything you type there is handled by the Google Play Store app under Google's terms and is never seen by Cropmark.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to SocialSure Private Limited through the developer contact details on the Google Play listing.

There is no server component.
