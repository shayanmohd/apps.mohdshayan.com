Rowmark Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Rowmark is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Rowmark collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. The app has no internet permission and no server, so nothing you enter can leave your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your projects, counters, the count journal, sessions, schedules, pattern files, highlights, yarn and needle entries, and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on the device. You can save all of it as a backup file from Settings (Save backup), restore that file on any phone, and delete everything by deleting a project in the app or by uninstalling Rowmark. The optional CSV journal export writes a file where you choose. Android's cloud backup may carry the app's data with your device backup under your own Google account settings; the unlock record is excluded from it.

Purchases

Rowmark is free, with one optional one-time unlock. The purchase is made in the Google Play Store app under Google's terms and Google's privacy policy. Rowmark only reads whether this Google account owns the unlock, keeps that answer on the phone, and sends nothing anywhere. A refund relocks the extra features and never deletes or hides what you made.

Permissions

Rowmark requests the following permissions:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: the haptic tick on every count and the buzz when a repeat or shaping step is due.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets the app ask the Google Play Store app to sell and confirm the one-time unlock.

Third parties

Rowmark contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The Google Play Billing Library and the Google Play In-App Review library are bundled; both talk only to the Google Play Store app on the phone, and the app's manifest removes the internet permission they would otherwise carry.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.
