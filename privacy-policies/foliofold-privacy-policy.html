FolioFold

Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. FolioFold is provided by SocialSure Private Limited.

What stays on your device

FolioFold keeps private working copies of PDFs you import, project names, source metadata, page order, rotations, inserted blanks, layout settings, export counts and dates, named presets and appearance preferences. It does not upload these records or operate an account system.

No app data collection

The application has no internet permission, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not receive your documents, project records, device identifiers, location or usage events from the app. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or broad storage access.

Files, printing and sharing

You choose documents and export destinations using Android's system document picker. A selected file provider may be a cloud service with its own policy. When you choose Share or Print, the receiving app or installed print service receives the file you select. Those services control their own storage, network traffic and retention. FolioFold does not silently send documents to them.

Flattened print exports contain rendered page images and may include sensitive content from your original. Original export copies the source unchanged. Complete archives include original PDFs, project settings and presets. Archives are not encrypted. Keep them in a location you trust and share them only with intended recipients.

Removal and backups

Remove a project in its Layout tab. Its private original is removed when no other project needs that copy. Files you saved outside FolioFold remain under your control. Restoring a valid archive replaces current projects and presets; invalid archives leave them intact. Appearance preferences remain local. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes local records. Export an archive first if you want to keep them.

Prepared PDFs and temporary rendering files are kept in the app's private cache and can be removed by Android. Saved or shared copies are independent of that cache.

Purchases and external pages

FolioFold is paid once through Google Play and has no in-app purchases. Google processes purchases and provides its own receipts, support and refund tools. We may receive aggregate sales and acquisition reports from Play Console. These are separate from document data, which the app never sends to us.

Opening Privacy or Support launches your chosen browser. This website is hosted through GitHub Pages on apps.mohdshayan.com. Its hosting provider receives ordinary web requests. Our pages bundle their fonts and images locally and include no analytics or advertising scripts.

Audience and changes

FolioFold is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If app data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.
