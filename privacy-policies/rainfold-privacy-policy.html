Rainfold Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Rainfold is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Rainfold collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

What is stored on your phone

Your saved mixes, the mix on the Listen screen and your settings are stored on your phone only. If you turn on the optional counts in Settings, the number of nights played and mixes saved are kept on the phone too and never sent. Export mixes in the app writes your mixes to a file you choose; uninstalling Rainfold or clearing its storage deletes everything it stored.

Purchases

Rainfold is free. One optional one-time purchase unlocks extra features. Google Play sells the unlock in the Google Play Store app under its own terms. Rainfold only asks the Google Play Store app whether this Google account owns the unlock and keeps that answer on the phone. Rainfold has no internet permission, so it sends nothing anywhere. For help with a purchase or a refund, write to shayanm2002@gmail.com.

Permissions

Rainfold requests the following permissions:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: keeps playback running with the screen off.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: the service type Android requires for audio the user started.
  • WAKE_LOCK: holds the processor awake only while sound plays.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets the app ask the Google Play Store app to sell and confirm the one-time unlock.

Third parties

Rainfold contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to SocialSure Private Limited through the developer contact details on the Google Play listing.

There is no server component.
