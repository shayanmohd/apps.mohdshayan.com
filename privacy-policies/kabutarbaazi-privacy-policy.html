Last updated 29 August 2026

KabutarBaazi is operated by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. It is written to match what the app actually does.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, advertising ID, or any analytics identifier. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

Your phone number

Your phone number is stored separately from your public profile and is hidden by default. It is revealed to a signed-in buyer only at the moment they tap the WhatsApp button on one of your ads. Every reveal is logged so we can detect and stop number harvesting, and reveals are rate limited. Your number never appears on your profile, is never sold, and is never shared with advertisers.

Tapping the WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp on your own phone. That is an action you take, not a transfer of your data to WhatsApp by us.

Photos and videos

Images are re-encoded on your phone before upload, which removes EXIF metadata including GPS coordinates. Outdoor photos of birds very often carry the location where they were taken, and we do not want the app to publish where you live. Videos are compressed on your phone before upload to keep data usage down.

Media is stored on Cloudflare R2 and served from kb-media.kamapathy.app.

Where your data is stored

The database is hosted by Supabase in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1). Media is stored by Cloudflare. Notifications are delivered by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Delete my account in the app. You can also use the web form without installing the app.

Removed immediatelyKept, and why
Profile, username, phone number, all ads, reels, posts, comments, messages, and every photo and video you uploaded Reports filed against your account are kept for 12 months with only a username snapshot. Deleting an account must not erase evidence of abuse. Moderation audit records are kept for the same period.

Because a conversation is a single shared record, deleting your account also removes that conversation for the other person. This is stated in the app before you confirm.

Children

KabutarBaazi is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
