Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Echo Rooms is an Android puzzle game published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy covers the app's gameplay and progress features.

Information on your device

The app stores room progress, movement history, best completed routes, hint counts and preferences in its private local storage. It does not require an account or ask for your name, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

No app data collection

Echo Rooms contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and has no application internet permission. It does not send gameplay records, identifiers or other app data to SocialSure Private Limited. It has no multiplayer, online leaderboard, live synchronization or developer-operated backend.

Backups and files you choose

Export and restore use Android's document picker. The app accesses the file you select or create; it does not request broad access to your device's files. A progress backup contains room histories, best routes and your selected room. Appearance and sound preferences remain on the device.

Backups are ZIP files with integrity checks, not encryption. If you save or share a backup through a cloud storage provider or another app, that service handles the file under its own policy. Echo Rooms does not automatically upload it. Android cloud backup and automatic device-transfer backup are disabled for the app.

Purchases and external links

Google Play processes the upfront app purchase and provides the download. Echo Rooms does not receive payment-card information. Google handles its store, payment, licensing and platform services under Google's policies. The app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Privacy and support links open your system browser only when you select them. Those pages are hosted on GitHub Pages at apps.mohdshayan.com and use no analytics, advertising scripts or external font services. Hosting providers may handle ordinary web request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, as part of delivering a page.

Deleting or moving your progress

Use Settings > Delete local progress to remove the game's room records and histories. Android's Clear storage or uninstall controls remove all app-local records and preferences. Files you exported elsewhere remain until you delete those copies. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to keep your progress.

Support messages

If you email support, your message and the details you choose to include are used to respond to your request. Avoid sending information you do not want included in that correspondence. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the same address, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Audience and policy changes

The app is offered to an adult audience and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
