Delete your account

The fastest way is inside the app: Settings → Delete my account. That removes everything straight away.

If you cannot open the app, fill in this form. We verify the request against the phone number on the account and delete it within 30 days.

What deletion removes

Your profile and username, your phone number, every ad, reel, community post and comment you made, all your conversations and messages, and every photo and video you uploaded.

What is kept

Reports filed against your account are kept for 12 months with only a username snapshot, so that deleting an account cannot erase evidence of abuse. Nothing kept identifies you beyond that username.
