Gearwake privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Gearwake is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy covers the Android app, package com.mohdshayan.gearwake.

Your workshop stays on your device

The app stores the current run, machine positions and buffers, choices, placement undo, finished-run records and preferences in private app storage. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, developer-operated server or application internet permission. All gameplay and daily-seed calculations run on the device.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or broad storage permissions. Optional sound and haptic feedback use the device's normal output functions.

Files you choose

The Android document picker grants access to a selected backup file or destination. A complete backup contains the current run, placement undo and finished-run history. Preferences remain on the device. The archive is validated before any existing records are replaced in a database transaction.

Backups are not encrypted. They may include seeds, challenge dates, results and completion timestamps. Store them where you are comfortable keeping this information. A cloud document provider may transfer selected files using its own network connection and policies. Gearwake does not operate that service.

Sharing results

When you choose Share result, Android's share sheet lets you select a recipient app. Shared text contains the outcome, credits, seed or daily date, workshop and rules version. It is sent only after your action. Recipient apps handle shared information under their own policies.

Purchasing the app

Gearwake is paid once before download through Google Play, with no in-app purchases. Google processes payment and store activity under Google's privacy policy. Gearwake does not receive payment card details. SocialSure may access order and aggregate sales information made available in Play Console.

Backup, retention and deletion

Automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Export a complete backup before changing phones or uninstalling. The app keeps up to 200 finished-run records, with a replay replacing the previous result for the same seed and workshop.

Delete local runs in Settings removes the current run and finished-run history. To remove all local records and preferences, clear Gearwake's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Delete exported files and shared copies separately. There is no server copy or account to delete.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the information you send and use it to answer the request. Please share a seed and a description of the issue instead of unrelated personal files. This website uses locally bundled fonts and images, with no analytics, external font CDN or cookies set by us. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs under its own policies.

Audience and changes

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. This policy will be updated if data practices change.
