Thirstday Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Thirstday is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Thirstday collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. The app has no internet permission, so nothing you enter can leave the phone through it.

What is stored on your phone

Your plants, their rooms and notes, each care task and its history, the photos you pick for a plant, saved window spots, light readings and day logs are stored in the app's own storage on your device. A photo you pick is copied into the app's private folder; the original is never changed. Android may include this data in your device backup under your own Google account settings.

You can save all of it to a file of your choosing at any time (Settings, Save backup file) and restore it on any phone. Deleting a plant removes its tasks, history and photos. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stored.

Purchases

Thirstday is free and sells one optional one-time unlock. Google Play sells the unlock in the Google Play Store app under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Thirstday only asks the Play Store app whether the Google account on this phone owns the unlock, keeps that answer on the phone, and sends nothing anywhere: the app has no internet permission. The answer is left out of backups: a new phone asks Google Play again. For a refund within 48 hours use Google Play; after that, write to the support address below.

Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: lets the app post the one daily watering digest on Android 13 and later. You can decline it; the Today list and the widget still show what is due.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: re-arms the daily digest alarm after the phone restarts.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets the app ask the Google Play Store app to sell and confirm the one-time unlock.
  • com.mohdshayan.thirstday.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: added by the AndroidX library so the app's own internal messages stay inside the app. It is never shown to you and gives no access to any data.

The light check uses the phone's ambient light sensor, which needs no permission. Readings stay on the phone.

Third parties

Thirstday contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The Google Play Billing Library is included only to talk to the Google Play Store app on the phone.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.
