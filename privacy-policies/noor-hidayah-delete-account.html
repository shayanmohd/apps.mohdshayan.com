Delete a Noor Hidayah account or data

Noor Hidayah and Noor Hidayah Partner · Updated 29 October 2026

You can request deletion of a Noor Hidayah pilgrim account and its associated personal data, or ask to remove specific data without deleting the whole account.

From the pilgrim app

  1. Open More, Settings, Account.
  2. Choose Delete account and read the information about records that may be retained.
  3. Confirm the request. You have 7 days to cancel before deletion proceeds.

Without the app

Email contactus@noorhidayahofficial.com with the subject Noor Hidayah account deletion or Noor Hidayah data deletion. Say whether you want the whole account removed or which information you want erased. Include the registered Indian mobile number so support can locate the account. Support will verify that you control the account before acting.

You can also open the secure account-deletion form. It requires a working sign-in code. If a code does not arrive, use the email route above; production message delivery is not yet enabled in the current testing service.

Do not send identity-document scans, Aadhaar numbers, passwords or one-time codes by email.

What is removed and what can remain

Routine profile details, contact details, the profile photo, personal progress, certificates and account access are removed within the service's 30-day deletion window after confirmation and the cancellation period. Shared chat contributions are emptied or anonymised as part of deletion.

Limited consent, audit, safety, request, tax and access-control evidence can remain. Some erasure schedules are not yet automated, as explained in the privacy policy. An operator's retained travel document and its trip link may remain until its own retention window closes. The automatic document-erasure schedule is still under legal review; you may request a specific document's erasure by email. Account deletion is not a promise to erase a record that must be retained for a stated legal or safety purpose.

Partner staff and operator records

If you use Noor Hidayah Partner, ask your operator administrator to close your staff access, or contact the support address above. The pilgrim self-service deletion form does not close a staff or operator account. To remove a prospective booking or a record your operator entered before activation, identify the operator in your request so it can be routed correctly.
