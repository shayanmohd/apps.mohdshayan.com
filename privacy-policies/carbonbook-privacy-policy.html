Carbonbook Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Carbonbook is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Carbonbook collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. There is no account and no server, and the app has no permission to use the internet.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage: your business details (name, address, tax number, bank details, terms), the logo and signature you pick, your customers and items, every invoice, quotation, receipt and other document you make, and the payments you record against them. The photo picker gives the app only the pictures you choose, so no photo or storage permission is asked for.

You get your data out whenever you want: back it up as one zip file through the system file picker, export it as CSV, or share, print or save any document as a PDF. Those files go only where you send them. To delete everything, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it.

Purchases

The one optional unlock is bought in the Google Play Store app, under Google's terms and Google's privacy policy. Carbonbook only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns the unlock, keeps that answer on the phone and sends nothing anywhere. Refunds go through Google Play, or through the support address below.

Permissions

  • com.android.vending.BILLING: lets the app ask the Google Play Store app to sell and confirm the one-time unlock.
  • com.mohdshayan.carbonbook.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: a permission scoped to the app itself, added by AndroidX so that no other app can send it broadcasts.

Carbonbook does not request the INTERNET permission, camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage.

Third parties

Carbonbook contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The Google Play Billing Library is used only to talk to the Google Play Store app on the phone.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes
