ClickTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026

ClickTrack is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. ClickTrack collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. Your click log is saved on your own device so it is still there when you reopen the app. It is never uploaded, it is not included in Android backups or transfers to a new device, it is cleared when you tap "Reset log", and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

ClickTrack requests no runtime permissions, so it never asks you to allow access to anything. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data off the device even in principle. You can confirm this on the Play Store listing under "App permissions". The only permission in the app is one that the AndroidX library adds for the app's internal use (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION). It keeps the app's own internal messages private to the app; you are never asked to grant it, and it gives no access to your data.

Third parties

ClickTrack contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.
