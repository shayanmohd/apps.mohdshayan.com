EchoDepths Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 August 2026

EchoDepths is published by Mohd Shayan. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. EchoDepths collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. Anything you enter is processed on your device, is never uploaded, and is gone when you close the app.

Permissions

EchoDepths requests the following permissions:

  • VIBRATE

Third parties

EchoDepths contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.
