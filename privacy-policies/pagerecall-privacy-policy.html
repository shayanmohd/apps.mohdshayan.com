PageRecall

Privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026. PageRecall is provided by SocialSure Private Limited.

Your study library stays on your device

PageRecall stores your decks, written questions and answers, PDF page links and selected regions, image masks, study plans, review history, saved sessions and editor drafts in private device storage. Imported PDFs and images are copied privately. We do not operate a server for these records and do not collect, receive or share your study content. The app has no account, cloud synchronization or automatic question generation.

Selected files

Android's document picker grants access only to files you choose. PageRecall does not request broad storage, camera, microphone, contacts, notification or location permissions. Originals are preserved byte for byte. Selected images or PDFs may already contain names, camera or location metadata; that metadata remains in the original and complete backup archives.

Optional Google Play purchase

One lifetime purchase adds unlimited PDF notebooks, image-occlusion creation, batch cards from selected PDF regions, configurable study plans and removal of ads. Google Play processes payment under its own terms. PageRecall receives purchase status and a purchase token through the Play Store service, acknowledges completed purchases and keeps a local ownership cache. It does not receive payment card details or send purchase records to a developer server.

Buying and restoring ownership require Google Play connectivity. Internet permission also supports ads and consent messages. Reviewer access is a separately documented credential; it does not charge money or change Google Play ownership.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free library overview can show contextual banner ads supplied by Google AdMob. We do not send study cards, PDF contents, images, notes, review history or exported files to AdMob. We request non-personalized ads. This does not eliminate all advertising data processing.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares your IP address, which can indicate approximate location; app and ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views; diagnostics such as performance, app launch time, hangs and energy usage; and device or other identifiers, including Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device. Google uses this data for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. SDK communications are encrypted in transit using TLS. There is no separate developer analytics service; Google SDK crash reporting is disabled, although advertising performance diagnostics are still processed.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks applicable privacy requirements and presents consent or opt-out choices where required before ads may be requested. When available, open Settings and choose Advertising privacy choices to change or withdraw your choices. Your records remain usable if you decline or if the consent service is unavailable. An ad may still be served in a limited form where permitted by your choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.

Once confirmed ownership is available, lifetime unlock removes banners. We check stored ownership before requesting consent for ads, and do not request ads while ownership is unresolved. Buying the unlock stops new ad requests; it does not erase advertising data already processed by Google. Google's privacy policy and advertising information describe its handling, retention and privacy controls. We do not control Google's retention periods or receive an individual advertising profile.

Exports and restoration

CSV and plain-text exports contain written card questions and answers; CSV also includes deck names and a human-readable source-page reference. Complete ZIP archives contain decks, cards, schedules, review history, study plans, saved sessions, PDF anchors and original PDFs or images. Archives exclude purchase ownership, reviewer credentials, advertising choices, application settings and unfinished editor drafts.

Exports are not encrypted. Anyone with access to an export can read its contents. If you select a cloud document destination or another app, that service handles the file under its own terms. PageRecall validates archive checksums, paths, record references and supported source files before replacing its saved library. Malformed archives leave the existing library unchanged.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them, replace the library with a backup, clear app storage or uninstall PageRecall. Deleting a notebook also deletes cards linked to it. Deleting a deck removes its cards and notebooks. Review history can retain anonymous local record identifiers and counts after a card is deleted. To remove all local history, clear app storage or uninstall. Original files outside the app and exported backups are not deleted automatically.

Android automatic backup and device transfer are disabled. Export a complete archive before moving phones. Clearing storage does not refund a purchase; ownership can be restored separately using the purchasing Google account.

Audience, links and changes

PageRecall is intended for adults. Scheduling is a study aid, not a measurement or guarantee of learning outcomes. Privacy and support links open your browser only when selected. Public pages contain no analytics or third-party font services; the hosting provider processes ordinary website requests.
