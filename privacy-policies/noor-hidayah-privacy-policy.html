Noor Hidayah Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

Noor Hidayah is published on Google Play by SocialSure Private Limited and developed for SOSA Consulting Services. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Noor Hidayah collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. Anything you enter is stored only on your device, is never uploaded, and persists locally until you reset the demo data or uninstall the app.

Permissions

Noor Hidayah requests no Android permissions. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data off the device even in principle. You can confirm this on the Play Store listing under "App permissions".

Third parties

Noor Hidayah contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to SOSA Consulting Services through the developer contact details on the Google Play listing.

There is no server component.
