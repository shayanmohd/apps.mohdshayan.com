This notice covers the pilgrim app, the Partner staff app and the connected operator service. For records an operator enters about a prospective traveller, the operator is responsible for its own notice and authority to enter the information. SOSA processes those records for the operator. Account consent is recorded when the pilgrim activates.

Information used by the service

  • Your profile: passport name, display name, Indian mobile, language and, where supplied, Saudi mobile, email, photograph, date of birth or birth year, gender and nationality. Your operator can initially save only your name and Indian mobile. These details support sign-in, travel arrangements and the operator's service.
  • Travel documents: passport and visa numbers and files supplied for your trip. Numbers and document files are encrypted separately on the server. Staff normally see a mask; an authorised reveal is recorded in the audit log. The service has no Aadhaar field.
  • Trips and practice: your groups, dates, itinerary, check-ins, announcement receipts, ritual progress, history and certificates. Religious practice is sensitive information. Operator staff and group leaders have different access; operators can see progress for the trips they manage. A certificate's public verification display follows its chosen name-visibility setting.
  • Safety: emergency contacts you provide and SOS events, acknowledgements and location updates during an open SOS. Staff see SOS events while their dashboard or Partner app is open. Noor Hidayah is not an emergency service.
  • Communication: filtered chat messages, reports, blocks and moderation records. The service removes recognised personal details before storing a chat message. If you enable contact discovery, phone numbers are hashed on your device; raw address-book numbers are not uploaded. Hashes are compared for the request and discarded, while match pairs and request counts may be kept.
  • Accounts and operations: sign-in sessions, devices, consent records, invitations, seats, support and data requests, audit records and limits against abuse. Operator and Partner accounts include staff identities and access roles. Operator enquiry tools record one prospective booking, its contact details, people count, package, travel month and notes.
  • Usage statistics: app event types, their hour, an identifier for the current app launch, app and platform version, language, font scale and offline status help us understand which features work. These events do not include your profile identity or message content. Usage statistics are on by default; you can turn them off in Settings, Usage statistics. The statistics have a 13-month deletion schedule.
  • Optional features: ratings, public operator listings and their photographs, and purchase or entitlement records when those features are enabled. Public listing information is visible to anyone. Payment-card details are handled by the app store, not collected by us.

Ratings and private comments

When you submit a trip review, operator stars are required. Stay, food and transport scores are optional. A public operator average and count appear only after at least three visible ratings; hidden ratings are excluded.

Comments submitted with the revised sharing notice can be read by active admins and agents of your own operator and the Noor Hidayah owner. Submitting through that form records your acknowledgement of the notice above the Send action. The operator's feedback view omits author identifiers, but the trip context or your words may identify you. Hidden comments are excluded from the operator's feedback view.

Comments without that acknowledgement, including earlier submissions and submissions from older clients, remain visible only to their author and the Noor Hidayah owner. Private comments are not shown in the public directory.

Permissions and your device

The apps use the internet for connected services. The pilgrim app asks before using location or contacts. Outside an open SOS, prayer times and Qibla use an on-device location or chosen city and do not send that position to our server. There is no background location tracking. Local biometric unlocking uses your device's system; we do not receive a fingerprint or face template. A chosen photo is re-encoded before upload. Offline copies can remain on a signed-in device and are cleared by the app's sign-out or data-clearing process.

The Partner app is for operator staff and does not request your phone's contacts or location. A paired watch, where supported, receives limited prayer, trip and SOS state from its phone. Google Play services may relay Wear OS state when the devices are not connected by Bluetooth.

Who receives information

Production account data is held with Supabase in Mumbai, India. The web service is hosted by Vercel. Operator staff receive only records their organisation is allowed to access. Group leaders have narrower trip access. Operator staff may access their own organisation's data while travelling, including from Saudi Arabia. Operators are responsible for their own travel and Nusuk Masar submissions.

Explicitly labelled sample-data builds use isolated test services in the United States. These builds accept invented records only, with no real trips or personal information. They are separate from the production service; production account data stays in Mumbai.

Features that require message delivery, push notifications, crash reporting or purchases can use Meta WhatsApp Business, MSG91, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Sentry, Apple or Google for that purpose. Some of those providers operate outside India. In the current release configuration, production message delivery and push notifications are not enabled, Sentry has no reporting configuration, and store purchases are disabled. We will update this notice when these services become active.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. There is no advertising SDK. These policy and support pages contain no analytics, scripts, remote fonts or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

The current retention rules are listed below. Legal review of the schedule is still pending. You may request deletion or correction using the contact and account-deletion routes below.

  • Profiles and account information are kept while the account is used. An account-deletion request has a 7-day cancellation period; routine account data is then removed within the service's 30-day deletion window, subject to the exceptions below. Completing a trip alone does not disable your account.
  • Passport access by operator staff normally ends 30 days after return, or 90 days with a recorded extension reason. A living account's passport record remains until account deletion or a separate erasure request. Visa erasure is intended after the trip's document window. The automatic document-erasure schedule is still awaiting legal review and is not enabled; contact support for an erasure request.
  • Trip records, including itineraries, announcements, check-ins and memberships, are scheduled for removal 120 days after return. A membership needed to link a retained document can remain with that document.
  • SOS records are removed 24 months after resolution, or 24 months after receipt when they have been acknowledged but not resolved. Events still marked open are not automatically deleted.
  • Group chat is retained for 90 days after return, direct messages for 180 days, and removed message content for 30 days. Reports and moderation records have a one-year schedule. Turning off contact discovery removes discovery and matching records.
  • Unclosed enquiries close after 12 months. Contact details, notes and the link to a pilgrim are cleared 180 days after closure. Anonymous enquiry counts remain for 5 years after closure for conversion reporting. Saved pilgrim contacts that have not been placed in a trip are not automatically purged after 12 months.
  • Export files are kept for 24 hours. Invitation records are retained for 90 days after expiry. Consent records have a 12-month retention period after account deletion, and audit records have a 13-month schedule. The intended retention for closed rights requests and an account tombstone without personal profile fields is 12 months; their automatic erasure is not yet implemented.
  • The intended retention for tax and transaction records, where applicable, is 72 months. Their automatic erasure is not yet implemented. Staff access-control evidence may remain for 12 months after closure. Records required for unresolved safety, legal or data requests may remain for their stated purpose.

Your choices and requests

Use More, Settings, Account in the pilgrim app to request an export, correction or deletion. You can edit your display name, language, Saudi mobile and email. Ask the managing operator to correct passport-related details. Optional permissions and consent choices can be withdrawn. For an account or data-deletion request without the app, use our deletion page.

Contact contactus@noorhidayahofficial.com for support or a data request. Do not email passport or visa scans, Aadhaar numbers, passwords or one-time codes. We verify identity before acting on a request.

Children

Child app accounts, verified guardian consent, dependant records and family-progress sharing are not implemented. A child account must not be activated in the current testing service. We do not automatically share progress with emergency contacts or family members. Tell support if you believe a child's information was entered without appropriate consent.
