OrbitLatch privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

OrbitLatch is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy describes the Android app, package com.mohdshayan.orbitlatch.

Game data stays on your device

The app stores puzzle attempts, completed results, custom puzzles and settings in private local storage. It has no app account, advertising, analytics SDK or developer-operated server. The app does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage-library permissions.

Google Play purchases

The optional lifetime unlock uses Google Play Billing through the installed Play Store app. Google handles payment information and purchase processing under its own privacy policy and terms. OrbitLatch receives purchase status and a purchase token to acknowledge ownership and restore the unlock. It stores a local ownership cache so confirmed access can remain available offline. It does not receive your payment card details or send purchase tokens to a developer backend.

SocialSure can access order and aggregate sales information that Google makes available in Play Console. Google may independently process store, billing and diagnostic information. See Google's privacy policy.

Files you choose to import or share

The Android document picker gives OrbitLatch access only to the file or destination you select. Imported puzzles and backups are validated before use. Exporting a puzzle or backup writes a file to your selected destination. Sharing a result opens Android's share interface; the selected recipient app handles anything you then choose to send.

Archives contain puzzle names, custom boards, progress and completion timestamps. They are not encrypted. Keep them private if their contents are sensitive. Purchase ownership is excluded from exports and restores.

Backup and deletion

Android cloud and device-transfer backup are disabled. Use the app's explicit backup export before uninstalling or changing phones. You can remove custom puzzles and attempts in Your puzzles. Removing an attempt retains its completed-result record. Clear OrbitLatch's app storage in Android Settings or uninstall to erase all its local records and preferences. Exported files and copies sent elsewhere must be deleted separately.

Clearing app data does not cancel Google Play ownership or delete Google's transaction records. Restore a valid purchase using the same Google account. Refund requests and Google-held records are managed through Google Play.

Daily puzzles

Daily puzzles are calculated from the UTC date on your device and bundled game content. No server request, location or unique user identifier is used.

Support and public pages

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. The public website uses no analytics, cookies set by us or third-party font CDN. Hosting providers may process ordinary request logs under their own policies.

Audience and changes

OrbitLatch is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
