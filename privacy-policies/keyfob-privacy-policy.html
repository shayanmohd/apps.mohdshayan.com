Keyfob privacy policy

Last updated 07 September 2026

Keyfob collects nothing. This page explains what that means in practice.

What is collected

Nothing. Keyfob does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that belongs to us.

Where your data lives

Your plan, your guardians' names and the letter you write are stored in this app's private storage on this device. Your recovery phrase is never stored: it is held in memory only while cards are being built, and it is gone the moment you leave that screen.

Network

Keyfob does not request the INTERNET permission. It cannot send your data anywhere, because Android will not let it open a network connection at all. This is verifiable in the app's manifest on the Play listing.

Permissions

  • VIBRATE. a short tap when a card is built or a drill step is completed

Children

Keyfob is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Analytics, ads and tracking

There are none. Keyfob contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash reporter. It does not use an advertising ID.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected or transmitted, there is nothing held about you to access, correct or delete on a server. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Any in-app export gives you a copy of your own content in an open format.

It is not a wallet

Keyfob never sees a blockchain address, a balance or a transaction, and it cannot move anything. It has no network permission, so it could not reach a chain even if it wanted to. It splits a phrase you already hold into guardian cards, and it writes down the plan for using them.

Your phrase

The phrase you type is turned into cards on the device and then discarded. Nothing about it is written to storage, to a log, or to any file the app produces. The cards themselves are yours to print or copy out; the app does not keep them either.

Guardians

You will type the names of people you trust. Those names live on your device only. Tell them they are guardians, because a plan they have never heard of is not a plan.
