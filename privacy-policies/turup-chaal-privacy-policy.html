Privacy Policy

The short version

  • ✓No ads and no ad tracking. The app shows no advertisements and collects no advertising identifier.
  • ✓No account, no sign-in. You never give us an email, phone number, or password.
  • ✓Play offline privately. Practice mode sends nothing off your device.
  • ✓Online uses only a display name + a random game ID to connect you to other players.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Turup Chaal card game ("the app", "we", "us") handles, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information the app handles

  • Display name. When you choose to play online, you enter a name shown to other players at the table. It is saved on your device and sent to our multiplayer provider only while you are in an online match.
  • Player ID. The app creates a random identifier on your device to keep track of your seat in an online room. It is not linked to your real identity and is shared with the multiplayer provider only during online play.
  • Connection data. When you play online, our multiplayer provider processes standard network information (such as your IP address) to connect you to the other players in your room.

The app does not collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or any payment information, and it requests no advertising identifier.

How we use it

The limited information above is used for one purpose: to run the game and connect you with other players in online matches. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Who it is shared with

  • Photon (Exit Games GmbH): the real-time service that powers online multiplayer. During online play it receives your display name, player ID, in-game moves, and connection data to relay the match. See the Photon privacy information.
  • Unity (Unity Technologies): the engine the game is built with. It may collect limited technical and crash-diagnostic data to keep the app stable. See the Unity privacy policy.
  • Google Play: distributes the app and may collect standard installation and diagnostic data under its own policy.

We operate no servers of our own, so there is no central database of players holding your information.

Storage and retention

Your display name and player ID are stored locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Online match data is transient: it exists only for the length of the game session and is not retained by us afterward.

Children's privacy

Turup Chaal is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Online connections are encrypted in transit by our multiplayer provider. No method of transmission is ever completely secure, but we keep the information involved to the practical minimum.

Your choices

  • Play Practice (offline) mode to share nothing off your device.
  • Use any display name you like online. It need not be your real name.
  • Uninstall the app to erase all locally stored data.
  • Contact us with any question about your data using the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and revise the effective date below. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
