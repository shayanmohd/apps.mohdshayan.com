Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Caseweave is an Android deduction game published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy covers its case notebooks, progress and preferences.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your place and time assignments, previous entry for undo, saved notes, evidence marks, revealed hint counts, completed cases, selected case and display or sound preferences in private local storage. It does not require an account or request your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

No app data collection

Caseweave has no application internet permission, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer-operated backend. It does not send notebooks, identifiers or other app data to SocialSure Private Limited. There is no live synchronization, multiplayer or online leaderboard.

Files and backups you choose

Export and restore use Android's document picker. Caseweave accesses only the file you select or create through that picker and does not request broad file-storage permission. A notebook backup contains saved entries, notes, evidence marks, revealed hints, completed cases, one-step undo information and the selected case. Appearance and sound preferences remain on this device.

Backups are ZIP files with integrity checks, not encryption. A cloud storage provider or another app you choose for an exported file handles that file under its own policy. Caseweave does not automatically upload backups. Android cloud backup and automatic device-transfer backup are disabled for this app.

Purchases and external pages

Google Play processes the upfront app purchase and provides the download. Caseweave does not receive payment-card information. Google handles its store, payment, licensing and platform services under Google's policies. The app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Privacy and support links open your system browser only when you select them. Public pages at apps.mohdshayan.com are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no analytics, advertising scripts or external font services. Hosting providers may handle ordinary web request information, including IP addresses and browser information, when delivering a page.

Deleting your records

Settings > Delete all notebooks removes local entries, saved notes, marks, hints and completion records. Android's Clear storage or uninstall controls remove all local app records and preferences. Exported files remain wherever you saved them until you delete those copies. Export a backup before uninstalling if you want to keep your notebooks.

Support correspondence

If you email support, the message and details you choose to include are used to answer your request. A backup can contain personal notes; send one only if you want support to see those notes. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the same contact address, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Audience and updates

The app is offered to an adult audience and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. This policy will be updated with a new effective date if the app's data practices change.
