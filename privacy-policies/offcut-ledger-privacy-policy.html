Offcut Ledger privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Offcut Ledger is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy covers the Android app, package com.mohdshayan.offcutledger.

Your workshop records stay on your device

Offcut Ledger stores projects, material dimensions, quantities, grain settings, notes, offcut locations and completed cutting plans in private app storage. Preferences are stored locally. It has no app account, advertising, analytics SDK, developer-operated server or application internet permission.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or broad storage permissions. It performs calculations on the device.

Files you choose

The Android document picker grants access to the file or destination you choose. CSV imports create project records. Backup imports are validated before replacing records in a single database transaction. Source files are not changed. Imported records are kept privately inside the app and do not require ongoing access to the original file.

PDF, CSV and backup exports write to your selected location. These files can contain project names, material details, notes, inventory locations and job timestamps. They are not encrypted. Keep copies private when their contents are sensitive.

Sharing and printing

Sharing a PDF creates a temporary report in private cache and opens Android's share sheet. Access is granted to the app you choose. Printing uses Android's print system and your selected print service. A recipient app, cloud document provider or print service may send data over its own network connection under its own policies. Offcut Ledger does not operate those services.

Temporary shared reports older than 24 hours are removed when you next share a report. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes the private cache. Files already exported or sent elsewhere are independent copies.

Purchasing the app

Offcut Ledger is paid once through Google Play before download and has no in-app purchases. Google processes payment and store information under Google's privacy policy. The app does not receive payment card details. SocialSure may access order and aggregate sales information made available by Google in Play Console.

Backup and deletion

Automatic Android cloud and device-transfer backups are disabled. Use Export full backup before changing phones or uninstalling. Backups include projects, offcuts and completed jobs; display preferences remain device-local.

You can delete individual projects, offcuts and history entries in the app. Deleting a project leaves its completed history intact. Deleting a history entry does not reverse the current inventory. To erase all local records, preferences and cached reports, clear Offcut Ledger's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Delete exported files and recipient copies separately. There is no server copy or account to delete.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive the information you send and use it to answer your request. Please send made-up dimensions and names when reporting a problem, rather than private workshop records. This website uses locally bundled fonts and images, with no analytics, third-party font CDN or cookies set by us. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs under its own policies.

Audience and updates

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. This policy will change if the app's data practices change.
