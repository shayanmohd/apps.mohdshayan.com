Doorway privacy policy

Last updated 05 September 2026

Doorway collects nothing. This page explains what that means in practice.

What is collected

Nothing. Doorway does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that belongs to us.

Where your data lives

Places, surveys, answers, photographs, lists, your needs lens and your last position are stored in this app's private storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account. A pack leaves the phone only when you export one yourself and choose where to send it.

Network

Doorway does not request the INTERNET permission. It cannot send your data anywhere, because Android will not let it open a network connection at all. This is verifiable in the app's manifest on the Play listing.

Permissions

  • CAMERA. to photograph a doorway, a toilet or a room while you are surveying it. The picture is downscaled and written to this app's storage on the device. It is never uploaded, because the app has no network permission at all
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION. to sort your places by how far away they are and to pin a place where you are standing. Android requires both to be requested together. The position is kept on the device and used only for that arithmetic
  • VIBRATE. a short tap when an answer lands or a place is marked as still accurate

Children

Doorway is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Analytics, ads and tracking

There are none. Doorway contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash reporter. It does not use an advertising ID.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected or transmitted, there is nothing held about you to access, correct or delete on a server. Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Any in-app export gives you a copy of your own content in an open format.

Your needs lens

The modules and thresholds you set describe your access needs, which is health adjacent information. Doorway keeps it local by architecture rather than by promise: it is never transmitted, and it is deliberately excluded from every pack you export, so sharing places with a friend never shares your needs.

Photographs

Doorway does not detect faces. It cannot honestly claim to, because it carries no model to do it with. Instead the review screen gives you a blur brush, and the pixels you paint over are destroyed before the photograph is written to storage. Photographs stay on the device unless you deliberately include them in a pack you export.

Packs you export

A pack is a plain JSON file containing the places, their surveys, and the name you chose to sign it with, which can be left blank. Your lens, your position and your settings are not in it. Once you send the file, where it goes is up to you and the app has no further part in it.

The sample high street

The eight places offered on first run are invented for demonstration. They are not real businesses, the measurements are not measurements of anywhere, and the whole sample can be removed in one tap.
