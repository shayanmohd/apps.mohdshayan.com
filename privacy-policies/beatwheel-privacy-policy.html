Beatwheel Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Beatwheel is published by SocialSure Private Limited. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. Beatwheel collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry and no identifiers. The instrument profiles, temperaments and drift sessions you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device, are never uploaded, and are removed when you uninstall the app. They leave the device only when you export a file or share one yourself.

If you buy the full unlock, Beatwheel keeps one yes or no answer on your phone saying whether your Google account owns it. That answer is never sent anywhere. Beatwheel also keeps a yes or no answer saying whether it was installed on this phone while it was a paid app, so that earlier buyers keep the full unlock. That answer stays on the phone too.

Permissions

Beatwheel requests the following permissions:

  • android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO (microphone): used only while the tuner is on screen, to measure the pitch of your instrument. The sound is analysed in memory and discarded. No audio is ever saved or sent.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING (Google Play billing): lets Beatwheel ask the Google Play Store app on your phone to sell the full unlock and to say whether your account owns it. You are never asked for it, and it gives Beatwheel no access to your payment details.
  • com.mohdshayan.beatwheel.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION: added by the Android support library so that messages Beatwheel sends to itself can come only from Beatwheel. It is private to the app, you are never asked for it, and it gives no access to anything else on your phone.

Purchases

The optional full unlock is sold by Google Play. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details under Google's own terms and privacy policy; Beatwheel never sees them. Beatwheel asks the Google Play Store app on your phone whether your account owns the unlock and keeps only that answer on your phone. Beatwheel has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Third parties

Beatwheel contains no advertising and no analytics. It includes the Google Play Billing Library, which Google provides for in-app purchases. That library contains a component that would send diagnostic logs to Google; because Beatwheel has no internet permission, the app cannot send them.

Children

Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to SocialSure Private Limited through the developer contact details on the Google Play listing.

There is no server component.
