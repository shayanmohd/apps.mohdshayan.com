PantryTurn

Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. PantryTurn is provided by SocialSure Private Limited.

Your records stay on your device

PantryTurn stores food names, quantities, locations, dates, notes, recipes, shopping lists, meal plans and remembered barcodes in the app's private storage. We do not operate a server for these records and do not collect, receive or share them. There are no accounts, ads, analytics SDKs or crash-report uploads.

Camera and notifications

The optional barcode scanner asks for camera permission when you open it. Camera frames are processed on your device, are not retained as photos and are not transmitted. A scanned barcode is added to a draft; saving it remembers the product name, unit and location locally. New products require manual entry. There is no online barcode lookup.

If you enable entered-date reminders, the app may ask for notification permission. Background work reads your local pantry dates and can show a daily summary. Android may delay reminders. The app does not request exact alarms, location, contacts or broad storage access.

Optional Google Play purchase

One optional lifetime purchase unlocks planning features. Google Play processes the transaction under Google's terms and privacy policy. PantryTurn receives purchase status and a purchase token through the Play Store service, acknowledges completed purchases and retains a local ownership cache. It does not receive your payment card information or send purchase records to a developer server.

Buying and restoring require Google Play connectivity. The app itself declares no INTERNET permission; Play Billing communicates with the Play Store app. The dependency's application telemetry cannot use an app internet connection. A separately documented reviewer-access credential does not make a purchase or change Google Play ownership.

Files you choose to import or export

Android's document picker lets you choose individual import and export locations. A complete archive includes your saved records and barcode memories. It excludes purchase ownership, reviewer access, settings and unfinished drafts. Archives and CSV/text exports are not encrypted. Anyone who can access an exported file can read its contents.

If you select a cloud document provider or share an exported file using another app, that service handles the file under its own privacy terms. This happens only at your direction. Importing an archive validates it before replacing the local records.

Storage, deletion and backups

Records remain until you delete them, restore a replacement archive, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Individual records can be removed from their detail screens. Barcode memories are retained locally until a replacement archive, clearing storage or uninstalling; removing a pantry record does not remove its remembered barcode. Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. You can create your own complete archive from Settings.

Clearing local storage does not cancel or refund a Google Play purchase. You can restore confirmed ownership through the Google account that purchased it. Deleting local data does not delete copies you exported elsewhere.

Links and audience

Privacy and support links open your browser only after you choose them. The website and browser then handle that visit independently. PantryTurn is intended for adults. Dates and quantities are user-entered records, not medical advice or food-safety assessments.
