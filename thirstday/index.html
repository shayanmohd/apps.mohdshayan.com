Hold the phone at the glass.

The light sensor reads the window in lux or foot-candles and places it on a ruler from 100 to 100,000 lux, then names the plants from your list and from about 230 houseplants that suit the spot. Every figure is an estimate from a phone sensor, not a lab meter.
