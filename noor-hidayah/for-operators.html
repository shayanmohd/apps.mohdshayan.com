Help your pilgrims stay informed.

Bring group details, travel plans and announcements into one operator workspace. Give pilgrims a clearer place to find their journey information.

Built tools, in a testing service

The operator dashboard supports pilgrim and group records, enquiries, itinerary publication and announcements. Noor Hidayah Partner gives invited staff access based on their role. The pilgrim app reads information received on the phone offline.

Public app-store launch and production group sync are still being prepared. We agree pilot scope before onboarding; we do not promise features or delivery dates that have not been confirmed.

Plan the journey in one place

  • Manage groups and the pilgrim records your organisation is authorised to enter.
  • Publish authored travel details with the reviewed ritual guidance shown in the pilgrim app.
  • Send group announcements and let authorised staff view assistance alerts while their dashboard or Partner app is open.

Start with a conversation

Email your organisation name, role and a short outline of what you need. We can discuss your operating model, confirm the available scope and agree the next onboarding or pilot step.

Do not send pilgrim lists, passports, visas or other personal documents in a marketing enquiry.

Data and responsibilities

Operators are responsible for the records they enter and the authority to enter them. Staff access follows assigned roles. Account consent is recorded when a pilgrim activates. See the privacy notice for current service facts, data retention and account rights.

Common questions

Which languages are supported?

The apps offer English, Hindi, Urdu and Kashmiri. Translations awaiting review are marked as first drafts.

Is there a public price or booking promise?

Partnership scope and commercial arrangements are discussed directly. This page does not quote a price, confirm a booking or offer travel services.

Where can pilgrims find an operator?

The operator directory is separate from this partnership page. A listing is not a promise of availability or a recommendation of a trip.
