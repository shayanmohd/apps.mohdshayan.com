For pilgrims

Guidance to take with you.

Prepare for your journey and keep useful guidance close, including when your connection is limited.

Join the launch list

Prepare and follow your progress

The app includes reviewed Umrah and Hajj guidance, duas, essentials and a personal progress checklist. Choose the Hajj journey that matches your arrangements. Ask a qualified scholar about your own circumstances; the app does not issue religious rulings.

Find your way and your prayer time

Makkah and Madinah map packs are bundled for offline use. Prayer times, Qibla direction and a tasbih counter are available on the phone. Check important times and places with your group leader.

Travel with your operator

Invited pilgrims can access operator-published itineraries and announcements when connected group services are available. Once received, journey information stays on the phone for offline reading. Signing in, receiving changes and sending messages or alerts need connectivity.

The service is in testing. Production group sync is being prepared, so this website does not promise live group updates to the public yet.

Read in your language

The app supports English, Hindi, Urdu and Kashmiri. Urdu and Kashmiri use a right-to-left layout. Translations awaiting review are marked as first drafts. Arabic appears in relevant religious content; it is not an app interface language. This website is currently in English.

Know where help goes

On an active trip, SOS alerts your operator’s staff. Staff see alerts only while their dashboard or Noor Hidayah Partner app is open. SOS does not call an ambulance or police, may not reach anyone without a network, and is not an emergency service.

For trip arrangements, contact your operator first. For app support, contact the support desk.

Availability

The apps are in testing and are not yet listed publicly in the app stores. No store download is offered here. Community is marked coming soon and is not available yet.
