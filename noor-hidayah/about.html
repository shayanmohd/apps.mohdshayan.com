Technology with a purpose.

We want pilgrims to feel informed, organised and connected, so they can focus on their worship.

Built around the journey

Noor Hidayah brings useful guidance, prayer tools, offline maps and operator-shared information into one companion. The product serves pilgrims and the organisations responsible for their travel arrangements.

Our current service is in testing. We describe the features available today and state where launch or connected services are still being prepared.
