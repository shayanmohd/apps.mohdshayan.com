Built around the needs of pilgrims.

Guidance without a connection

The bundled ritual guide, duas and map packs work offline. Sign-in, new group updates and sending assistance requests need a connection.

Four app languages

English, Hindi, Urdu and Kashmiri are available. Translations remain marked as first drafts where review is pending. This website is in English.

Clear data responsibilities

Your operator manages the records it enters. Access depends on the person’s role. The privacy notice explains the information stored, retention and your choices.

Read the privacy notice

Assistance, with clear limits

During a trip, SOS sends an alert to your operator’s staff. It does not call emergency services or guarantee a response. For urgent help, contact local emergency services directly.
