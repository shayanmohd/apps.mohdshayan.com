Before you start.

Can I use it without internet?

Your inventory works offline. Ads, consent messages and Google Play purchases need connectivity. Confirmed lifetime ownership remains available offline.

What happens if I change phones?

Save a complete inventory archive from Settings, copy it to the new phone and restore it. Android automatic backup is disabled. Restore the purchase separately through the Google account that bought it.

Does Keepstead verify warranties or values?

No. Dates and values are records you enter. Keepstead is not an insurance, valuation or warranty verification service. Android can delay reminder notifications.

Can other people scan my labels?

The QR contains a local item identifier. It opens no public record and contains no address, notes or value. Read a label image from Keepstead's inventory search on a phone with the matching inventory.
