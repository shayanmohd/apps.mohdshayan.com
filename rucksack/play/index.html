Elsewhere

Everything on this map is walked.

There is no fast travel, no boost to buy and no way to skip a kilometre. The only thing that moves your wanderer is your own feet, on ordinary days, in the gaps of an ordinary life.

The road

3,500 kilometres. Eleven regions. An ending.

From Fenwarden's Gate to Cael-on-the-Mountain. Most walkers take a year. Some take two. The road does not mind, and it does not reset at midnight.

How it counts

Your steps, converted honestly.

1,300 steps make one kilometre. A single day converts at most 40 kilometres, so a long walk counts and a car journey mostly does not.

Rucksack never uses location, and it has no way to reach the internet. It reads a step count and nothing else.

About

Rucksack reads one number from this phone: the step counter built into it. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot upload anything, and it never asks for location. The weather at your camp is Elsewhere's own weather, worked out from the date and the region you are standing in.

Free, finished, and not a subscription. There is nothing to buy inside it.
